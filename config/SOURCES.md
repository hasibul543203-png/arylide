- “Layout Plan of Jolshiri Abashon” (PDF, RAJUK-hosted object storage): <https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-rajuk/2024/12/67b22fb2a496448ca78014e163ffb9f0.pdf>
- Source figures used: 2,133.300 acres (863.319 hectares / 6,453.26 bigha); estimated population 628,000; net residential area 722.25 acres (33.86%); gross residential area 1,854.95 acres (86.95%); community-service area 1,132.70 acres (53.10%); parks/lakes 357.59 acres (16.76%); and residential roads 612.99 acres (28.73%).
- The detailed table and legend also list schools, colleges, health facilities, community centres, markets, CBD, religious services, play areas, parks/lakes, several road widths, pedestrian and cycle routes, water and sewerage facilities, solid-waste facilities, safety services, and other civic uses.
- The PDF says that the plan map belongs to RAJUK and may not be copied or issued without written permission. The website links to the source instead of embedding or reproducing the map.
