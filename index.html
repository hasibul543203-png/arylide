THE PLACE · OFFICIAL LOCATION NOTES

Jolshiri, read in context.

A planned township east of Dhaka, located in Rupganj Upazila, Narayanganj. Here is the location story published by Jolshiri Abashon itself.

Click schematic for directions Schematic · not to scale

EASTERN DHAKA · RUPGANJ

Between two rivers.
Beside a growing city.

The official Jolshiri website places the township south of RAJUK Purbachal New Town and east of Bashundhara Residential Area, across the Balu River. It describes the project as lying between the Balu and Shitalakshya rivers. Directions and travel times can change—confirm your route before visiting.

Jolshiri site states~1.3 km south of Purbachal New Town
Access route statedKuril → Purbachal 300 ft Link Road
Administrative locationRupganj Upazila, Narayanganj
Waterfront contextBalu River · Shitalakshya River
See the published route-distance notes

Jolshiri Abashon’s website states approximately 7 km (about 8 minutes, as published on the site) from Kuril Flyover via the Purbachal 300 ft Link Road; it also lists route distances of about 21 km and 15.6 km from Dhaka Zero Point via two different approaches, and about 7.5 km from the American Embassy. These are published reference distances, not live traffic estimates.

WHAT THE OFFICIAL LAYOUT SHOWS

Planned around a whole community.

The RAJUK map legend includes civic, educational, commercial, recreational, transport, and utility uses. The list below describes what the plan marks—not a promise of completion.

01 / LEARNING & HEALTH

Schools, colleges & care

Nursery and primary schools, secondary schools and colleges, a university, small clinics and hospital uses appear in the published plan.

02 / OPEN SPACE & PLAY

Parks, lakes & recreation

Urban greens, Eidgah, lakeside green, parks, play lots, sports facilities, golf-course and amusement-park uses are marked on the layout.

03 / BUSINESS & RETAIL

CBD & neighbourhood commerce

Central Business District blocks, commercial plots, corner shops, markets, Katcha Bazar, a super mall and a five-star hotel are included in the map legend.

04 / COMMUNITY & FAITH

Shared civic spaces

Mosques, temple/church sites, a central mosque and Eidgah, community or convention centres, clubs, and a graveyard are identified as planned uses.

05 / UTILITIES & SAFETY

Water, power & public services

Water treatment, deep tube wells, electric substations, sewerage lift/treatment, solid-waste facilities, firefighting, police, filling stations and traffic stands.

06 / MOVEMENT & ACCESS

Roads, bridges & walkways

Primary, secondary and tertiary roads, bus bays, pedestrian bridges, footpaths, cycle tracks and lakeside walking routes appear in the official legend.

Technical notes and map-sheet references for developers
Road right-of-way labels on the layout

The map shows several typical widths, including 42.5 m (139.4 ft), 30 m (98.43 ft), and 18.5 m (60.7 ft). Confirm the exact road adjoining a plot from the authorised plan and current field survey.

Map-sheet / administrative context

The published plan places Jolshiri in the eastern portion of the DMDP Detailed Area Plan and references sheets L12, L13, M12 and part of M13, Group A.

RS mouzas listed in the PDF

Baghber, Baro Berayed, Bayerachhoni, Dakshin Nabogram, Gajaria, Gutiab, Horinagram, Keyaria, Majhina, Mushurigram, Naora and Rupganj.

Utility legend abbreviations

CBD: Central Business District; DTW: Deep Tube Well; SLS: Sewerage Lift Station; SWTS: Solid Waste Transfer Station; SIS/PIS: social/physical infrastructure; UG: Urban Green.

Implementation / BOT note

The official Jolshiri website refers to a Build–Operate–Transfer approach for services such as power, water treatment and supply, sewerage treatment, and education or health establishments. Check the authority site for any current procurement or partnership process.
